Terms & Conditions

Standard Commercial Terms

  1. Prices: Ex-works, exclusive of GST @18%, freight, octroi/entry tax, and insurance. Prices valid for 30 days from date of quotation or until 31 August 2025, whichever is earlier.
  2. Payment: 50% advance with order confirmation, 40% before dispatch, 10% within 7 days of installation completion.
  3. Delivery: 4–6 weeks from advance receipt for standard sizes. 6–8 weeks for custom dimensions or premium finishes.
  4. Warranty: 12 months from date of installation against manufacturing defects in workmanship and materials. Excludes consumables, flooring, glass breakage from misuse.
  5. Standard sizes: Prices cover cabin dimensions as listed in the dimension table. Non-standard sizes attract 8–15% surcharge (A9).
  6. Raw material escalation: If SS, MS, or glass prices move more than ±8% from the date of quotation, prices are subject to revision.
  7. Flooring, COP/LOP panels, electrical fittings, and installation are not included unless separately quoted.
  8. Jurisdiction: Pune, Maharashtra. Disputes subject to Pune civil courts.

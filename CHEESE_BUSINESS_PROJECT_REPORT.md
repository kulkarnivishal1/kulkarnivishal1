# Detailed Project Report — Artisanal & Fresh Cheese Micro-Enterprise (Badlapur)

**Full formatted report:** [`Cheese_Business_Project_Report.docx`](./Cheese_Business_Project_Report.docx)

A weekend-to-full-time cheese-making business plan for an SC/ST category woman
entrepreneur, built around land she already owns in Badlapur (Thane district,
Maharashtra), designed to be run on weekends with her daughters while she
keeps her current job, before transitioning to full-time.

## What's in the report

1. Executive summary and honest reality check on the profit target
2. Promoter profile and why SC/ST + woman status unlocks extra scheme support
3. Why Badlapur (own land, milk access, connectivity to Mumbai/Thane) is an advantage
4. Mumbai Metropolitan Region cheese market analysis (size, growth, existing brands, pricing)
5. Product portfolio — fresh, quick-turnaround cheeses only (no aging rooms, no capital lock-up)
6. Machinery & minimum-capital equipment list (~₹7 lakh plant & machinery)
7. 12-month implementation timeline, weekend → full-time
8. **Government schemes & subsidy matrix** — PMEGP, CMEGP, PMFME, Stand-Up India,
   CGTMSE, NSFDC Mahila Samriddhi Yojana, NSTFDC Adivasi Mahila Sashaktikaran Yojana,
   Maharashtra SC/ST Investment Fund, MAVIM/Nav Tejaswini, Udyam, ODOP
9. Licenses & regulatory compliance (FSSAI State License, Udyam, GST, trademark, etc.)
10. Project cost (~₹12 lakh) and means of finance (~95% from subsidy + guaranteed loan)
11. **Year 1 month-by-month P&L** — weekend-only operation, every month profitable,
    ≈ ₹9.9–10.5 lakh net profit for the year
12. **Year 2 month-by-month P&L** — full-time operation at ~500-550 kg/month,
    ≈ ₹21 lakh net profit — this is where the ">₹20 lakh/year" target is actually reached
13. Financial ratios (DSCR, ROI, break-even), risk analysis, marketing plan, SWOT
14. Annexures: key government contact points and scheme-application document checklist

## Key honesty note

At the requested starting capacity of 200–300 kg/month (weekend production), a
realistic Year-1 net profit is **~₹10 lakh** — every month profitable, none at a
loss. Reaching **₹20 lakh+/year** genuinely requires the Year-2 full-time scale-up
to ~500-550 kg/month shown in Section 12, not the weekend-only starter phase.
The report is built this way deliberately, so the numbers can be trusted and used
in real bank/scheme applications.

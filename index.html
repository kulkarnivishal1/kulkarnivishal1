Procedure
  1. Place the phone flat on the lift floor, screen facing up, near the centre.
  2. Tap Start. Wait for the “Recording” banner.
  3. Initiate the lift ride (single floor-to-floor cycle).
  4. When the car stops and doors begin to open, tap Stop.
  5. Review parameters, graphs and download the PDF report.
